GEMINI MODELS AND FEATURES

1. Gemini 3.8 Flash
Model ID: gemini-3.8-flash
Features: Advanced reasoning, software development, coding, agentic workflows, and complex tasks.

2. Gemini 3.7 Flash
Features: Coding, complex tasks, agent workflows, and multi-step execution.

3. Gemini 3.6 Flash
Features: Fast responses, multimodal understanding, and general AI tasks.

4. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite
Features: Fast processing, cost-efficient responses, and high-volume AI applications.

5. Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite
Features: Efficient processing, low-cost workloads, and fast AI responses.

6. Gemini 3.1 Pro
Features: Advanced reasoning, complex problem-solving, coding, and agentic applications. Availability may depend on preview status.

7. Gemini 2.5 Pro
Model ID: gemini-2.5-pro
Features: Complex reasoning, advanced coding, difficult problem-solving, and multimodal understanding.

8. Gemini 2.5 Flash
Model ID: gemini-2.5-flash
Features: Fast responses, reasoning, high-volume tasks, and multimodal inputs.

9. Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite
Model ID: gemini-2.5-flash-lite
Features: Low latency, cost efficiency, and high-throughput applications.

10. Gemini 3.8 Live
Features: Real-time voice conversations and low-latency interactive voice applications.

11. Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS
Features: Text-to-speech, expressive voice generation, and voice design.

12. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe
Features: Speech-to-text, language detection, speaker identification, and word timestamps.

13. Nano Banana 2
Features: Image generation and image editing.

14. Nano Banana Pro
Features: High-quality image generation, detailed layouts, and text rendering in images.

15. Gemini Omni Flash
Features: Video generation, video editing, keyframe interpolation, and synchronized audio.

16. Gemini Embedding 2
Features: Multimodal embeddings for text, images, video, audio, and PDFs. Useful for semantic search and RAG systems.

17. Gemini Embedding
Model ID: gemini-embedding-001
Features: Text embeddings, semantic search, similarity matching, and retrieval-augmented generation.

IMPORTANT:
Features and availability depend on the exact model and API version. Some models may be in preview or have limited access. Check official documentation for the latest details.
